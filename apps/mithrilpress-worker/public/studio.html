      HOSTNAME MANAGEMENT

      Connect a customer domain

      The site subdomain remains available while a customer hostname is prepared. MithrilPress verifies ownership, provisions its TLS certificate, and activates the hostname after the CNAME is visible. DNS changes on a customer's provider are never made automatically.

        Custom domains are not enabled on this hosting deployment. The default MithrilPress subdomain continues to work.

        OWNER-GUIDED DNS TRANSFER

        Plan a nameserver migration

        MithrilPress verifies domain control, compares private BIND zone snapshots, prepares a destination import file, and checks public nameserver and DNSSEC propagation through Cloudflare and Google public resolvers. These providers receive DNS lookups for this domain after ownership is proven. You keep control of both DNS providers and make all provider and registrar changes yourself. Keep the source zone active through the rollback window.
